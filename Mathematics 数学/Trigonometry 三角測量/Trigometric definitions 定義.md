- **If the acute angle *θ* is given**, then any right triangles that have angle *θ* are similar to each other
	*This means that the ration of any two side lengths depends only on θ*
### <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Tangent</mark>
$tanθ = \frac{opposite}{adjacent}$

### <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Sine</mark>
$sinθ = \frac{opposite}{hypotenuse}$

### <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Cosine</mark>
 $cosθ = \frac{adjacent}{hypotenuse}$

### <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">Cosecant</mark>
 $cscθ = \frac{hypotenuse}{opposite}$

### <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Secant</mark>
$secθ = \frac{hypotenuse}{adjacent}$

### <mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">Cotangent</mark>
$cotθ = \frac{adjacent}{opposite}$
